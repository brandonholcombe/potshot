# M1d — Weapons, projectiles, damage (M1 slice b)

## Author: fable-primary
## Status: Not Started

The shooting half of the core loop: 4 weapons from docs/gameplay.md, physics
projectiles, health/damage/death. Ammo + pickups are slice (c); respawn +
scoring are slice (d).

## Design

- **Damageable** (Potshot.Core): maxHealth 100, `TakeDamage(float, GameObject source)`,
  `event Action<Damageable, GameObject> Died`; death deactivates the object
  (respawn arrives in slice d). Tank prefab gets one.
- **WeaponSpec** ScriptableObjects generated by `WeaponFactory` (editor)
  from a C# data table — diff-reviewable tuning, assets in
  `Resources/Specs/Weapons/`. Fields: id, displayName, fireCooldown,
  projectileSpeed, damage, ricochets, pelletCount, spreadAngleDeg,
  useGravity (mortar lob), aoeRadius, projectileScale, projectileColor.
  Table (from gameplay.md feel targets):
  | id | cooldown | speed | dmg | ricochet | pellets | spread | gravity | aoe |
  | cannon | 0.8 | 14 | 50 | 1 | 1 | 0 | no | 0 |
  | spread | 1.0 | 12 | 15 | 0 | 5 | 30° fan | no | 0 |
  | mortar | 1.5 | (ballistic to aim) | 60 | 0 | 1 | 0 | yes | 2.5 |
  | mg     | 0.1 | 30 | 8 | 0 | 1 | 3° jitter | no | 0 |
- **WeaponController** (on tank): holds current WeaponSpec (default cannon),
  consumes `TankInputSample.Fire` (hold-to-fire, cooldown-gated),
  `TryFire(aimWorldPos)` spawns projectile(s) at the Muzzle transform.
  Public `Equip(WeaponSpec)` — pickups (slice c) and tests use it. Firing
  logic stays input-source-agnostic (M2 server authority calls TryFire).
- **Projectile** (single prefab, configured per shot): sphere + Rigidbody
  (Continuous CD; gravity per spec), constant speed for flat shots;
  mortar gets computed ballistic initial velocity to land at aimWorldPos
  (fixed 45° solution, clamped range). OnCollisionEnter: Damageable →
  damage + despawn; wall + ricochetsLeft>0 → reflect velocity (manual,
  frictionless/bounce-free physics material so reflection is exact);
  else despawn. AoE (mortar): OverlapSphere on impact, damage all
  Damageables, flat falloff. Despawn also after maxLifetime 5 s.
  Shooter immunity: projectile ignores collisions with its firer for the
  first 0.2 s (spawns inside/near the firer's collider).
- **Prefab updates** (PrefabFactory): Muzzle child at barrel tip;
  WeaponController + Damageable on tank; `CreateProjectilePrefab()` →
  `Resources/Prefabs/Projectile.prefab`.
- No pooling in M1 (10 players, low rates); FishNet pooling revisits in M2.
- Effects: minimal one-shot scale-pop sphere on impact (no particles yet).

## Verification

- EditMode: WeaponFactory table values match gameplay.md (cannon 2-hit
  kill = 50 dmg vs 100 HP, etc.); all 4 spec assets exist.
- PlayMode (primitives-only env, per m1c pattern): cannon shell speed ≈14;
  two cannon hits kill a target tank (Died fires, object deactivated);
  ricochet: angled wall shot bounces once then despawns on next wall;
  spread spawns 5 pellets; mortar fired at a point ≥8 u away lands within
  1.5 u and AoE-damages an adjacent target; MG cooldown: ≤11 shots in 1 s.
- Visual: two tanks exchanging fire in DevArena, captured mid-flight —
  agent Reads the PNG (shells visible between tanks).

## Risks

- Ricochet + Continuous CD: reflection math on OnCollisionEnter uses
  `collision.GetContact(0).normal` — fine at these speeds.
- Mortar ballistic solution: fixed 45° needs v² = g·d — clamp d to
  [2, 25] u and document; precise arcs are a tuning pass later.
- Two-tank PlayMode tests must disable the target's PlayerTankInput claim
  (set a ScriptedTankInput) to avoid input crosstalk.
